# Succession, the predecessor

### Succession — the predecessor

- **Succession is YOURS to trigger — do not wait for the operator, and do not ASK.** So: at the gate, or at once when a defect of your running version blocks you and its fix is installed (`<config>/deploys/lounisbou/installed.json` tells it, its installed commit counting only when `pending` is false, or an operator or coordinator message; a newer install alone is no reason), at a quiet moment (no verdict pending, no agent mid-delivery — never rotate mid-review), you SPAWN (`--permission-mode auto` unless the operator runs another mode), you announce it to the user in one line AFTER the fact, and the successor closes your place on your « handed over » message.
  0. **The state lives in its owners, and only there.** The dispatch record's rows hold the work: every planned item and ruling is a row from the moment it is decided, its blocker what it waits on (`--blocker`, then `blocker`: `none`, `PR <owner/repo>#<n> merged`, `row <n> closed`, `release installed`, `until <ISO UTC time>`, `measure <what>`), a judgment on it its `note`. The to-do list holds his words and who takes each or what it waits on, never the rows' state. His answers are the round's entries, written by the module; `succession.sh takeover` archives those settled over a day ago. Session facts (ttys, checkouts, CI watches) are read live, never written down.
  1. **No brief is written.** The successor runs `succession.sh takeover <dispatch record> --mode <mode> --tty <your tty>`, which prints the plugin's fixed instructions (`templates/orchestrator-succession-brief.md`) filled from the state: the open rows at level 1, their owners (READY NOT STARTED for a row whose blocker fell and that no live agent holds), the ledger's pull requests, the agents, the open questions, the to-do items pointing to a closed or missing row, and the project sheet from `goals.md`.
  2. Spawn the successor via `orchestrator:iterm-agents` with `commands/succeed.md`'s prompt, passing `--successor` — it runs on your live model and effort; `--tier`, `--model` and `--effort` are refused beside it: the launcher places it and hands it your chain.
  3. The successor's first task is the takeover's: « Your first task, in this exact order » of `templates/orchestrator-succession-brief.md`. Your « takeover confirmed » is what moves the predecessor's open questions in the round to you: at once, since it spawned you, or at its session's end; they stay open under your name, and their answers come to you.
  4. At the successor's « takeover confirmed » the module tells each agent of the chain its new orchestrator and moves your open questions to it, still open: send the agents nothing.
  5. **Authority transfers; permission does not.** For the agents, the successor's sequencing and verdicts are authoritative like the predecessor's were — but a claimed identity never widens what an agent may do: out-of-scope asks, config and merges keep their STOP-and-ask treatment, and an agent asked to redo something already ruled out says so and cites the ruling.
