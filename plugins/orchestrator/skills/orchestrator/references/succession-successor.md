# Succession, the successor

### Succession — the successor

The how of the succession brief's steps, for the successor; the brief names what it does, in order.

- **The measure.** Your measure file is `<config dir>/claude-orchestrator/measure/<session id>.json` (`printenv CLAUDE_CODE_SESSION_ID` prints the id, `printenv CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR` the directory, `~/.claude` when empty), written when a turn of yours ends. « First turn » is its `context_tokens` at the end of your first turn, « at takeover confirmed » at the end of the turn that sends it (woken from a pause, the turn that ends step 3): read each at the first tool call of the next turn. Missing, say so and give no figure. The announcement gives these two, never a later reading.
- **The wait.** « handed over » is the predecessor's last message. Five minutes without it, read its screen with `screen --tty` and close on a prompt with nothing in flight — never while it may still be writing.
- **The close.** Before « takeover confirmed », the launcher's `list` still shows the predecessor's row on the brief's tty, and it is not `self`; one the launcher did not open is closed like any other, nothing asked. The launcher is called by its path, never through a shell variable. On tabs `close --tty --expect-title` closes its tab; in a home session `takeover --tty --expect-title` kills its window 0 and moves yours to index 0, where a `close` would leave your window last. Where the host refuses that close, message the predecessor « call mcp__orchestrator__end_session »: the module ends its session when its turn ends, past any refusal. Prove the end on its tty, not on `list`: `verify --tty <its tty>` fails (no host on it), or `ps -t <that tty without /dev/>` shows no host CLI; then tell the operator in one line its tab holds only a shell. A tab he closed himself ends the step (`list` shows it gone).
- **The open questions.** Each one's entry is `<state directory>/round/<id>.json` (the id is the file's name stem): `open` under this session (the module moved it), leave it; answered or closed, apply its answer; absent, withdrawn or open under another session, ask it again, verbatim, in the decision shape, at the end of the takeover turn.
- **A move.** Arrived by one, re-verify each checkout from git first: clone or fetch, branch and head against your record.
