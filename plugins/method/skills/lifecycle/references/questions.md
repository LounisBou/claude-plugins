# The question

One question per message, asked once, in this shape:

```
**Question i of N**
_Subject_ : <a few words>
_Context_ : <two to six sentences: what is at stake, what is already known>
_Question_ : <one sentence, ending with a question mark>
_Choices_ :
- **A** : <the choice> — cost: <…> — gain: <…>
- **B** : <the choice> — cost: <…> — gain: <…>
_Recommendation_ : A — <the reason>
```

- Two to four choices, lettered from A.
- When the host offers a question dialog, the same parts go into it: the choices as its
  options, the recommended one first and marked as recommended.
- The answer is recorded where the work keeps its decisions, with its date, and is not asked
  again.
