# The question

One question per message, asked once, in this shape:

```
**Question i of N**
_Subject_ : <a few words>
_Context_ : <two to six sentences: what is at stake, what is already known>
_Question_ : <one sentence, ending with a question mark>
_Choices_ :
- **A** : <the choice> — cost: <…> — gain: <…>
- **B** : <the choice> — cost: <…> — gain: <…>
_Free answer_ : <you may answer in your own words instead>
_Recommendation_ : A — <why it beats the others>
```

- Two to five choices, lettered from A to E.
- The free answer is always open; its text never opens on a lone letter.
- The recommendation argues its letter against the others; a bare letter is none.
- Name every item, in the context and in each choice, in plain words (what breaks, for whom,
  what it costs him), plus its pull request with the repository name. A row number of a
  tracking table may follow in parentheses, never alone.
- A host dialog takes the same parts: the choices as its options, the recommended one first
  and marked; the free answer is its own « Other ».
- The answer is recorded where the work keeps its decisions, dated, and not asked again.
